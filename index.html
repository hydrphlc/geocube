The GEOCUBE Invert Tool

The goal was to create an easy to use invert tool with a variety of configurations to be more versatile than any other invert product on the market. Designed to speed up the inverting process by allowing direct instrument shots on pipes. Also, finally achieve a quick accurate way to find pipe sizes utilizing total station precision. Our product specializes in getting inverts with the help of range poles, total station, prism, and hand level. By utilizing tools already in a surveyor's toolbox, inverting can become a simple, clean, and sanitary process.

Utilizes the power of a total station and simple math for quick work of inverts.




Benefits of this product:

  • Endless configurations and multiple probe rod angles.
  • Configure for extremely deep or extremely recessed inverts, by just adding rods to the configuration.
  • Configure for tight quarters, by just switching to a 1ft or 2ft tipped probe rod.
  • Achieve cleaner sanitary sewer inverting.
  • Get inverts where angle readers cannot by maneuvering obstruction.
  • Probe and measure irregular recessed pipes.
  • Achieve a more accurate way to get pipe sizes.
  • Ability to measure elliptical pipe size through stakeout mode
  • Improve safety with this product. There is less need for field crew to go inside structures.
  • Option to use with prism and instrument or no prism and instrument.
  • Have less confusion and less mistakes. Have more consistency.
  • Product is designed to be as simple and low tech as possible.
